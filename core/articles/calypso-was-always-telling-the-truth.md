The kitchen turntable raised me before any pulpit did, and it was spinning calypso.

Conscious calypso, to be exact, the truth-telling tradition, the one that sang the social commentary, that put the real news of the working man's life into a melody so it could not be silenced. My auntie sang along to every line. And for years I thought that world and the world of the gospel were two separate countries, and that becoming a woman of the Word meant leaving the first one behind. I was wrong, and *Calypso Consciousness* is the album where I finally said so.

## The Instinct Was Always Reaching for a Name

Here is what I heard when I finally listened as a teacher instead of a nostalgic: the calypsonian's deepest instinct, *tell the truth, no matter who it discomforts*, was never opposed to the gospel. It was reaching for it. **Yeshua** said, *ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free* (John 8:32). The whole conscious-calypso tradition believed the same thing the Scripture believes, that truth is liberating, that a lie keeps people bound, that somebody has to sing the real thing out loud. That instinct came from somewhere. On **Truth in the Trinidad Lyric,** I finally name where:

> Calypso always told the truth. Father, the same tradition now sings Your Son.

The lineage did not need to be abandoned. It needed to be brought home to the One it had been unknowingly reaching for the whole time.

I open the album with **Mind of Christ on the Shuffle,** and I mean the title exactly. Paul writes, *be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind* (Romans 12:2), and then makes the staggering claim a few letters later, *we have the mind of Christ* (1 Corinthians 2:16). The renewing of the mind is not the erasing of your culture. It is the redeeming of your consciousness. A Trinidadian believer walking with the mind of **Yeshua** does not stop being Trinidadian. She thinks His thoughts *in* the shuffle, not instead of it.

That is the reclamation this whole record is doing, taking the consciousness that calypso trained and handing it its true Subject.

## Every Instrument, Every Island

There is a stubborn idea that certain sounds are too worldly to carry worship. Psalm 150 dismantles that idea in six verses: *praise Him with the sound of the trumpet, praise Him with the timbrel and dance, praise Him upon the loud cymbals.* The list is not a fence. It is an open invitation, and Trinidad answered it with the tenor pan. So on **Holy Spirit on the Tenor Pan** I put the instrument where it belongs, in the offering. *Father, You receive the tenor pan.* Every people brings its own instrument to the throne, and none of them arrive as second-class.

And I did not stop at the instrument. I reclaimed the whole procession. **Carnival for the Lord** reaches back past the costume to something older, the memory that a procession can be an act of worship. When *David danced before the Lord with all his might* (2 Samuel 6:14), stripped of his royal dignity in front of everyone, he gave us permission for the body itself to praise. The Trinidadian heart that pours into the road already knows how to do that. I am simply pointing the parade at the throne.

The album also has a hymn on it, and I chose it on purpose. **Amazing Grace on the Shuffle** carries the melody the whole diaspora knows, but I sing it remembering that the man who wrote those words wrote them out of his own turning from the slave trade. That is real history, and I hold it honestly. The same grace that turned that ship's captain crossed the same ocean that carried the stolen, and met the descendants of the stolen on the Caribbean shore, and it is *the same grace* still. *Same grace then, same grace today.* When a steelpan plays that melody on a Sunday morning in Port-of-Spain, the whole terrible and redemptive weight of that water is being sung over into something free.

## The Wisdom Was Already Walking the Islands

The boldest thing I say on this record is on **Wisdom Walked the Caribbean,** and I want to be precise about it because it is easy to misuse. When the missionary boats came, some arrived with the assumption that they were *bringing* God to a godless place. But Scripture names **Yeshua** as *the wisdom of God* (1 Corinthians 1:24), the One *in whom are hid all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge* (Colossians 2:3). The Wisdom of God is not a Western possession that had to be imported. Christ was Christ before any steamship sailed. **Yahuah** was already sovereign over those islands, already the sea's Lord, already the One toward whom every genuine longing for truth was straining, long before the colonial chaplaincy arrived.

I do not say that to erase the missionaries who genuinely carried the good news. I say it to heal a wound, to tell the Caribbean believer that she was never outside the reach of the Wisdom of God, and that her hunger for truth was itself evidence He had already been near.

> Holy Spirit knew the islands first, and now the islands know Him better.

## Bright Joy Marches Home

The album ends where the whole story ends. Isaiah promises that *the ransomed of the Lord shall return, and come to Zion with songs and everlasting joy upon their heads* (Isaiah 35:10). **Bright Joy Marches Home** is that verse with a brass band, the diaspora, the ransomed, marching from the panyard to the altar to the throne, joy on our heads like a crown, the whole scattered family coming home. Isaiah even foretold the sound: *Sing unto the Lord a new song... from the ends of the earth,* from *the isles* (Isaiah 42:10, 12). The islands were written into the choir from the beginning.

## Who I Made This For

I made *Calypso Consciousness* for the one who thinks their culture and their Christ live in separate rooms. For the calypsonian, the panman, the artist who suspects their gift is too worldly to be wanted. For the diaspora believer who was told, in a hundred quiet ways, that God arrived on the islands by boat and might still consider them a mission field rather than a home.

Hear me. Your consciousness was made for **Yeshua.** The truth you were always trying to tell has a name, and He is the Truth Himself. The Wisdom you were reaching for walked your waters before anyone taught you His name. So bring the whole tradition home, the pan, the shuffle, the truth-telling lyric, the joy on your head, and lay it at the throne where it was always headed.

Calypso was always telling the truth. It just took me a while to hear Who the truth was about.
