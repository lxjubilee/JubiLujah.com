There is a lie I carried a long time up the mountain, and I want to set it down where you can see it.

The lie is this: that a people are lost until someone hands them a book. That the Creator waits at the edge of the map, arms folded, until the right voice arrives with the right pages. I was raised on the far end of that story, the end where you are supposed to feel grateful you were found, and quietly ashamed of everyone who came before you. This whole album is me climbing back down to tell you the truth I finally heard in the thin air: **Yahuah** was already speaking. He was speaking your Name and His own long before the ship was ever sighted from shore.

## Why I Had to Make This One

I did not want to write an argument. I wanted to write a homecoming.

For years I met people, good, faithful, weathered people, who believed their grandparents were in the dark. That the prayers their great-grandmothers lifted at the river bend went nowhere, because the "right words" had not crossed the ocean yet. And every time, something in my chest broke a little, because I do not believe in a God who is late.

The apostle Paul stood on a hill in Athens, looked at an altar inscribed *to an unknown god*, and did not say, "You are lost." He said, in effect, the One you have been reaching for in the dark, *Him I proclaim to you* (Acts 17:23). He told them that in **Yahuah** we live and move and have our being (Acts 17:28). Paul did not bring God to Athens. Paul named the God who was already there.

That verse is the spine of this record. I built twelve songs on it because one was not enough to undo a lifetime of being told you were a stranger to your own Maker.

## What the Title Means

*The Word Was Always Speaking.* Not "started speaking." Always.

John opens his Gospel with the oldest sentence there is: *In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God* (John 1:1). Then, four verses of glory later, *the Word became flesh* (John 1:14). The same Word who spoke light into the dark spoke over the high plateau, over the tall grass turning at the crease, over every people on every shore. Paul told the Colossians that in this Son *all things hold together* (Colossians 1:17). If that is true, and I have staked my life on it, then there was never a square foot of this earth where the Word was silent.

So the opener, *The Voice the Wind Carried*, is not a metaphor I invented for a pretty melody. It is a confession: the syllable the wind had spelled was the Name no scribe had yet held. The breath before the book was the same breath.

## The Heart Behind the Songs

Let me take you inside three of them, because the doctrine only becomes a home when it has a face.

*They Knew You Before the Ship Sighted Shore* is about a grandmother walking at dawn with a child on her hip, turned toward a fog thicker than the weather knew, carrying a Face she had not seen yet. And underneath the whole song runs the deeper truth that undoes all our pride: *You knew them first. You wrote the note.* We think our reaching is the beginning. It never is. The reaching is already an answer.

Then there is the song this article is named for, and the one I can barely sing without stopping.

> Grandfather whispered Yeshua in his sleep, 
> a Name no one in the family had taught.

I wrote it around an old man at the end of his days, three nights unopened, and then his mouth shaping a sound older than English and older than the old speech, **Yeshua**. The Hebrew name of the Son. A Name he had never heard pronounced, never seen spelled. And the Scripture I laid it on is Joel's promise that *your old men shall dream dreams* (Joel 2:28). I am not asking you to believe every detail of one man's passing. I am asking you to believe in a Spirit who can lay a Name on a tongue no preacher ever reached. Because if He can do that, then no one you love is beyond His speaking.

*The Vision the Elder Saw in 1825* stands on the ledge above the run, where an old man sat ten mornings without food, waiting. The storm came, and the voice was not in the storm. The earthquake came, and the voice was not in it. The fire came, not there either. This is Elijah on Horeb, exactly (1 Kings 19:11-12). *And after the fire a still small voice.* I put my people on that same mountain because the mountain is my lane, and because the God who met Elijah in the thin quiet is not a regional God. He speaks in the low whisper on every high place where a hungry heart will wait for Him.

## The Ground It Stands On

I am careful here, so hear me clearly. I am not telling you the old ways were secretly Christianity. I am not baptizing everything a people ever did or believed. Scripture is the judge of all of it, mine included.

What I am telling you is what Hebrews tells me: *Long ago, at many times and in many ways, God spoke*, *and in these last days he has spoken to us by his Son* (Hebrews 1:1-2). Many times. Many ways. One Son. The morning mercies did not begin the day the message arrived by boat; Lamentations says His compassions are *new every morning* (Lamentations 3:22-23), and mornings are older than any of our arrivals. That is why *Before Contact, You Were Faithful* had to be on this record. He was faithful every dawn that ever broke over the dry land, whether or not anyone yet had the breath to say His Name aloud.

And one day, Revelation shows it, *a great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, tribe, people, and language*, stands before the Lamb (Revelation 7:9). Every tongue a different door, and He walks through all of them. Your grandmother's tongue is one of those doors. It always was.

## Who This Is For

This album is for the one who was made to feel like a latecomer to their own Father's house.

It is for the person who loves **Yeshua** with everything and still carries a quiet grief that the people who raised them "missed it." Hear me: *We Were Not Lost. You Were Already Speaking.* The altar you did not have a Name for was already His. The prayers the wind carried were never lost. He held every one in His hand, the way John saw the golden bowls of incense that are the prayers of the saints (Revelation 5:8).

And it is for the worn-down believer standing on their own high ledge tonight, hungry, waiting through the storm and the fire for a word, sure everyone else got the loud dramatic sign and you got silence. Friend, the silence *is* where He speaks. Keep waiting on the thin still air. The Name is already in the room.

The closer, *The Cloud of Witnesses Sings Too*, is where I finally let the whole mountain sing at once, the grandfather who whispered, the elder who saw, the great-grandfather whose prayer rose like smoke, all of them at the table now, none of them gone, none of them silent (Hebrews 12:1). When you lift your voice, you are not starting the song. You are joining one that has been climbing since before the first ship, and will not stop when the last star burns down.

You were never lost to Him. You were already being spoken. Come up the mountain and hear it.
