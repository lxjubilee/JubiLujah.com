The world handed me bad news with a serious face. The anchorman frowning like a frown could win the race. The cynic on the corner saying *nothing ever change.* And for a long stretch of my life I agreed with him. I figured the smart people were the sad ones, and joy was just what you had before you found out how things really are.

Then I met the actual Truth, and He was *grinning.* He walked into the room I had already decided was dark, and He was not wringing His hands. So I made a record to answer every serious face and every corner cynic, not with a bigger argument, but with a smile they could not explain and could not wipe off.

**Truth Wears A Smile** is that record. It is a rapso album, Trinidadian calypso groove under spoken-word commentary, steelpan lead, iron and brass and a big crowd shouting back. Calypso was always the music of the sharp-tongued street commentator. I just redeemed the tongue.

## What the title means

*Truth Wears A Smile.* Not truth *pretends* to smile, not truth *forces* a smile through gritted teeth. The truth itself is smiling, because of how the story actually ends.

Here is the whole thesis, straight off the opening track:

> They sell me the panic, they sell me the gloom, but You walk grinning, Lord, right into the room.

Most people think faith is the frown you put on to seem serious about God. I think that gets it exactly backwards. **Yeshua** said, *"I am the way, the truth, and the life"* (John 14:6), and the same **Yeshua** told His followers, *"In my presence is fulness of joy"* is what David sang of Him (Psalm 16:11). Nehemiah stood in front of a weeping people and told them to stop, because *"the joy of the LORD is your strength"* (Nehemiah 8:10). Joy is not the reward for getting through the truth. Joy is what the truth is *made of.*

## Why I had to make it

Because cynicism is the cheapest costume in the world, and I wore it for years, and it nearly cost me everything.

There is a track called "Cynic Turned Believer," and another called "The Comedian's Confession," and another called "Same Joke, Sober Now", and they are all the same man, which is to say they are all a version of me. The mocker who used bad news as material. The comedian who could make a whole room laugh and go home empty. What changed the mocker? The answer the crowd shouts back is the only one that ever works: *Jesus changed the mocker.*

I had to make this album because the church too often hands the world the truth with the exact serious face the world was already drowning in. And meanwhile the enemy gets to look like the fun one. That is a lie, and I wanted to name it. **Yeshua** told us who invented lying in the first place: the devil, *"a liar, and the father of it"* (John 8:44). The very first lie in the Book was a question dressed up as concern, *"Yea, hath God said?"* (Genesis 3:1). Every gloom the world sells you is a descendant of that first lie. The Light exposes it: *"In him was life; and the life was the light of men. And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not"* (John 1:4-5).

## The songs, and the sound of the grin

"Steelpan Says His Name" hands the melody to the instrument itself, that bright, unmistakable Caribbean shimmer answering back the moment I call out to **Yeshua.** "Loud in the Daylight" refuses to keep the good news whispered in the shadows, because He said it plainly: *"Ye are the light of the world… let your light so shine before men"* (Matthew 5:14-16). Light is not shy, and it is not for after dark only.

"Who Told You That Lie" is the detective song. It takes the panic the world sold you and traces it back to the liar who first wrote it. "Light Switch in My Chest" and "Grin That Won't Come Off" are the testimony of what happens next: the switch flips, and the grin will not come off, no matter how hard the old cynicism tries to scrub it. That is not naivety. That is Galatians 5:22, *"the fruit of the Spirit is… joy"*, growing where it was planted.

And "Bad News Ran Out of Road" is my favorite, because it is Paul's taunt in a calypso hook. When the resurrection is true, the bad news finally hits a dead end: *"O death, where is thy sting? O grave, where is thy victory?"* (1 Corinthians 15:55). The worst news the world has, the grave, ran out of road at an empty tomb.

### The rule I sing by

There is a line in the opening track that is the ethic of the whole album, and I hold to it:

> No angry finger pointing at the man, just point at the Lie and hold out Your plan. I call out the liar, but I love the human.

That is the calypso wit redeemed. The street commentator's job was always to name what is wrong. I just refuse to name a *person* as the enemy. I name the *lie,* and I love the human every time. Truth with a grin does not shame you into the light. It disarms you into it.

## Where the carnival ends up

The album climbs toward "Carnival of the Redeemed," and I built that one straight out of Revelation, *"a great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues"* (Revelation 7:9). Because that is the real party at the end of history: every culture, every steelpan and every drum, one enormous carnival of people the bad news could not keep. And the closer, "Last Laugh Belongs to Jesus," is exactly what it says. The cynic thought he had the last word. He doesn't. **Yeshua** does, and it is a laugh.

## Who this album is for

This one is for the cynic on the corner. For the comedian who goes home empty. For the exhausted believer who thinks holiness means never smiling again. For anyone who has been handed the truth with such a grim face that they assumed the good news must be bad news too.

It isn't. The Man from Galilee walked out of the grave. The Light is here and the dark cannot dim it. So I don't do the frown, and I don't do the fear. I grin at the darkness, because the story already ended, and it ended well.

Go ahead. Try to wipe the grin off. You can't. It won't come off. That is what the truth does to a face.
